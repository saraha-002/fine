5. Booking Tips for Social Events

  • Book in advance. Social events require preparation – the companion needs to know the dress code, venue, and schedule. Contact at least a few hours ahead, or a day ahead for weekend events.
  • Be clear about the occasion. Tell her: “It‘s a dinner date at The Mavericks, smart casual dress code, 7 pm.” Or: “Private party at an Airbnb, casual but presentable.”
  • Agree on the rate and inclusions. Confirm whether the fee covers dinner/drinks or if you will pay separately. Transport fees should be discussed upfront.
  • Meet at the venue or arrange pickup. For a first social date, meeting at the restaurant is safest. For repeat bookings, you may arrange to pick her up.
  • Respect her boundaries. A social event companion is not automatically available for intimacy afterwards unless discussed and agreed. If you want the evening to extend, communicate that clearly before booking.
  • Tip well for exceptional social chemistry. If she made your evening memorable, a tip of KSh 1,000–2,000 is appreciated.
