How to book safely in Syokimau

  • Never pay the full amount upfront – a small deposit (KSh 500‑1,000) is okay for serious therapists. Full payment after service.
  • Check for red flags: No address until you pay, stock photos only, prices too good (e.g., KSh 1,000/hour – almost always fake).
  • Ask about hygiene: Do they use fresh sheets? Is there a shower? If they hesitate, skip.
  • Bring your own oils if you have sensitive skin – coconut or almond oil is safe.
