Imara Daima Signature Mall Evening Experience – 2025 Night Guide

27 May 2025 | Brian Omondi | 6 min read

Popular escorts in Imara Daima

Clarification: “Signature Mall” is the official name of the shopping complex in Imara Daima. It is also widely referred to as “Imaara Mall” after the estate. This guide covers the same facility – a modern retail and entertainment hub along Mombasa Road.

Imara Daima has grown rapidly over the past few years, and its main shopping centre has become a landmark for residents and visitors alike. Signature Mall (often called Imaara Mall) is a modern complex along Mombasa Road, about 25 kilometres south of Nairobi’s CBD and close to JKIA. If you are in the area in the evening – whether for shopping, a casual meal, or just to pass time – here is what you need to know.

1. Overview – What Is Signature Mall?

Signature Mall is a mixed‑use retail and entertainment complex developed by Canon Aluminium Fabricators Ltd. Construction began in January 2016 at a cost of approximately US$11 million (around KSh 1.14 billion). The mall opened in 2018 and has since become a key commercial hub for the fast‑growing communities of Syokimau, Mlolongo, Athiriver and Kitengela[reference:0].

The mall covers a gross lettable area of 259,300 square feet and accommodates over 100 tenants, including retail shops, restaurants, offices and a hotel. The anchor tenant is Choppies Kenya, one of the largest supermarket chains in East Africa[reference:1].

2. Evening Atmosphere – What to Expect After Dark

Signature Mall is open daily from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Imaara Mall (the same facility) is listed as open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM[reference:2]. Most shops close by 9–10 PM, but restaurants and the on‑site hotel operate later.

In the evening, the mall has a modern, spacious design with wide walkways and bright lighting. It does not feel deserted, but foot traffic noticeably decreases after 8 PM. The atmosphere is calm and relaxed – more suited for a quiet dinner than late‑night partying.

3. Dining Options – Where to Eat in the Evening

Food lovers will find a variety of dining options at Signature Mall[reference:3]:

  • Pearl View Restaurant – Offers a range of cuisines in a comfortable setting.
  • Tibs Ethiopian Restaurant – Authentic Ethiopian dishes, including injera and tibs.
  • Wamka Barbecue & Grill – Grilled meats, barbecue, and local favourites.
  • Pizza Inn and Big Knife – Popular casual dining options inside Imaara Mall[reference:4].
  • Chandarana Foodplus – A supermarket with a deli section and ready‑to‑eat meals.

Reviewers consistently praise the food quality. One recent review notes: “The food was delicious and fresh. I will definitely come back again”[reference:5]. Another highlights the “inviting ambiance, warm service, and flavorful, well‑executed dishes” at CJ’s Imara Daima, a newer addition to the mall[reference:6]. For a quick and popular option, the shawarma at Big Knife comes highly recommended[reference:7].

4. Security – Staying Safe at Night

Security is a top priority at Signature Mall. The mall was built with a state‑of‑the‑art fire detection and fire‑fighting system, surveillance systems (CCTV), and 24‑hour security personnel[reference:8]. The facility has parking space for over 600 vehicles in the basement and outdoors, all monitored by security staff. However, general safety precautions still apply: avoid carrying large amounts of cash, do not flash expensive jewellery, and if you are walking outside the mall after dark, stick to well‑lit main roads rather than isolated backstreets.

5. Transport – Getting to and from Signature Mall

Matatu (Cheapest Option)

Indimanje Sacco is the main matatu operator on this route. If you are in Nairobi CBD, the pick‑up and drop‑off point is Bus Station (BS) next to Afya Centre. The final destination is St Bakhita, with drop‑offs at Imara Daima Estate among others. These matatus use Mombasa Road, then Tegla Lorupe Rd and Cosmos Ndeti Rd. The fare is approximately KSh 100. The journey takes 45 minutes to 1 hour during peak time and 20‑30 minutes during off‑peak hours[reference:9]. If you are travelling from Mombasa by SGR, board any matatu at the SGR stage opposite Hilton Garden Inn and ask the conductor to drop you at the Imara Daima footbridge. From there, a boda boda will cost you only KSh 50 to your destination in Imara Daima[reference:10].

Train (SGR / Commuter Rail)

The morning train leaves Imara Daima Railway Station for town at 7 am and takes approximately 30 minutes to get to town. In the evening, the train leaves town at 5 pm for Syokimau station with drop‑offs at Imara Daima Railway Station. The ticket price is usually KSh 40 to and from Nairobi Town[reference:11]. Note that the last train is around 9 PM, so for later returns you will need a taxi or Uber.

Uber / Bolt

For convenience and safety, Uber or Bolt is the best option, especially after 9 PM. From the CBD to Signature Mall, expect to pay KSh 600–1,000 depending on traffic and time of day. From JKIA, the fare is around KSh 500–800. Ride‑hailing apps are widely available in the Imara Daima area, though wait times may be slightly longer after midnight.

Boda Boda

Available but only recommended for short distances during daylight. For evening travel, stick to taxis or ride‑hailing apps.

6. Accommodation – Hotels Inside the Mall

If you are staying overnight or planning a late evening visit, Signature Mall has two notable hotels on site:

  • Azure Best Western Hotel – A 3‑star hotel occupying the top two floors of the mall. It has 70 elegantly‑designed, fully‑furnished rooms and serviced apartments, targeting business travellers, lay‑over passengers, airline crews and leisure travellers. Operated under the Best Western franchise, it offers quality accommodation with convenient access to the mall‘s shops and restaurants[reference:12].
  • PrideInn Plaza Nairobi Airport – Located on the 4th floor of Signature Mall, this hotel offers direct access to the mall‘s shopping, dining and entertainment options. It is a popular choice for travellers needing proximity to both the airport and the mall[reference:13].

These hotels are excellent options for late‑night visitors – they allow you to enjoy the mall‘s evening facilities and then simply walk to your room.

Final Tips for a Smooth Evening Visit

  • Arrive by 8 PM if you plan to shop – most stores close by 9‑10 PM.
  • Parking is ample but can get crowded on weekends. The mall has over 600 parking spaces including basement and outdoor areas.
  • If you are meeting someone at the mall in the evening, suggest a public spot like the food court or the main entrance near the security post – well‑lit and safe.
  • For return transport after 10 PM, pre‑book an Uber or Bolt. Wait times can be 10‑15 minutes.
  • Carry small notes (KSh 200, 500) – boda drivers and some shops may not have change for larger denominations.
