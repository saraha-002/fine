Where to Park Safely Near JKIA Airport – 2025 Guide

28 May 2025 | Brian Omondi

Popular escorts near JKIA

Why this guide matters: For many travellers, where to leave their car safely can be one of the biggest concerns. If you‘re driving to JKIA, this guide gives you the facts – official rates, hotel alternatives, a critical security alert, and a practical checklist.

JKIA handles millions of passengers annually, and many of them drive themselves to the airport. But parking at JKIA is not straightforward – official rates can add up quickly, security has faced scrutiny, and there are better alternatives for longer trips. This guide covers all the official and private parking options near JKIA as of 2025, including rates, security features, and a critical warning you need to know.

1. Official Parking Options at JKIA

JKIA has three main car parks: short‑term, long‑term, and a multi‑storey garage. The airport‘s official parking facilities have a total capacity of 2,299 parking spaces. The multi‑storey car park alone is designed to accommodate approximately 1,500 vehicles. These facilities are monitored 24/7 by CCTV and security personnel.

If your trip is longer than a few days, parking at an airport hotel is often a more affordable and secure solution. For as low as KSh 400 per day, you can enjoy peace of mind and often get a free shuttle to the terminal.

5. JKIA Parking vs. Alternative Travel – What‘s Best for You?

Though this is a parking guide, it is worth considering whether driving is the best option for your situation.

JKIA Parking is ideal if:

  • You live within the Greater Nairobi Region and driving is more convenient than public transport.
  • Your trip is short (1–3 days) and the parking cost is negligible compared to your travel budget.
  • You plan to arrive at the airport very early or very late when public transport is unreliable.

Alternative travel is better if:

  • Your trip will last a week or more – the total parking fees become significant.
  • You live along a major matatu or train route that connects directly to the airport.
  • You prefer to avoid the stress of Nairobi traffic and navigating through airport security.

6. Final Checklist – How to Park Safely at JKIA

Before you drive to JKIA, run through this checklist:

  • Step 1 – Assess your needs: How long will you be away? Do you value speed and convenience over cost? Can you travel via public transport?
  • Step 2 – Choose your parking option: For short trips (1–3 days): official airport parking is more convenient. For long trips (7+ days): hotel parking is almost always cheaper and just as safe.
  • Step 3 – Prepare your vehicle: Lock all doors and close all windows. Remove all valuables or lock them in the boot out of sight. Take a photo of your car‘s parking spot and its location.
  • Step 4 – Know how to pay: If you use official parking, remember the USSD code `*566#` for cashless payment. Keep some cash or your M‑Pesa app ready in case of technical difficulties.
  • Step 5 – For extended parking, consider a hybrid approach: Park at a safe, free or low‑cost residential street in Syokimau or Mlolongo, then take a short 5‑minute boda boda or taxi to the terminal. This is a common tactic for locals, though you assume the risk of street parking.
Expressway tip for drivers: If you are parking at JKIA, the Expressway can save you significant time. The JKIA exit is well‑signposted. Toll for a saloon car (Class 3) from Syokimau to JKIA is approximately KSh 170, from Westlands to JKIA around KSh 410. Factor this into your travel budget.
