🚗 Transport & Access

Imara Daima is located approximately 8‑10 km from JKIA and 8 km from Nairobi‘s CBD. It is well connected via Mombasa Road, the Eastern Bypass, and the Nairobi Expressway.

  • From JKIA: 15‑20 minutes via Mombasa Road (Uber/Bolt KSh 500‑800).
  • From CBD: 20‑30 minutes via Expressway (toll KSh 410‑500) or Mombasa Road.
  • Matatu (Indimanje Sacco): From CBD (Afya Centre) to Imara Daima – KSh 100, 45‑60 min.
  • Train (SGR / Commuter Rail): Imara Daima Railway Station – KSh 40 to Nairobi CBD, last train ~9 pm.

🏘️ Gated Communities – Security & Privacy

Imara Daima is known for its secure, well‑planned estates. Most short‑stay apartments are inside gated communities with 24‑hour security.

  • Kings Millennium Apartments – 24‑hour private security, CCTV, electric fence, gym, pool, lift access. One of the most upscale options.
  • Villa Franca Estate – gated with police post nearby, spacious apartments, quiet environment.
  • Imara Daima Estate (general) – mix of gated and open areas; private guards on most blocks.
  • Acacia Estate – modern, CCTV, guest‑friendly (adjacent to Syokimau).

For maximum discretion, choose an Airbnb with self check‑in (lockbox entry).

🌙 Nightlife & Dining – Imaara Mall & Beyond

Imara Daima‘s nightlife revolves around Imaara Shopping Mall (often called Signature Mall).

  • Imaara Mall – open 8 am – 10 pm. Has restaurants (CJ‘s, Pizza Inn, Big Knife), a supermarket (Naivas), and a cinema.
  • Azure Best Western Hotel – on top of the mall, 3‑star, 70 rooms, bar/lounge.
  • PrideInn Plaza Nairobi Airport – 4th floor of Signature Mall, bar and restaurant.
  • Nearby spots: Sheba Lounge, local nyama choma joints along Mombasa Road.

After 10 pm, options are limited – most residents prefer private indoor meetups or short drives to Syokimau or Kitengela.

🛡️ Safety & Security – What You Must Know

Imara Daima is one of Nairobi‘s safer residential areas, but visitors should still be aware:

  • Gated communities provide excellent security – 24‑hour guards, CCTV, and access control.
  • There is an active police post – Villa Franka Police Post and Imara Daima Police Post.
  • Critical fire safety alert: In September 2025, a massive fire at Katani Villas (near Imara Daima) destroyed multiple homes. The area lacks functional fire hydrants. When booking an Airbnb, ask the host about fire extinguishers and emergency exits.
  • Avoid isolated backstreets near the railway line after dark. Stick to main roads and well‑lit areas.
