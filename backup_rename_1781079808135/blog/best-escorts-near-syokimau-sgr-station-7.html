You step off the Madaraka Express from Mombasa, tired but not too tired. You don't want to drag your bag to Nairobi. You want a discreet meet within walking distance of the SGR station. Traveler note: The station is officially "Syokimau Railway Station" (SGR). It's 5 minutes from Gateway Mall. Most incalls are off Namanga Road or near Acacia Estate.

1. Lucy – incall 5 min from station (KSh 3,500/hour)

Lucy's apartment is a 5‑minute walk from the station exit (take the footpath behind the parking lot). It's a clean single room with a bed and shower. She's fast to respond – replies within 5 minutes on WhatsApp. She offers massage + full service. No deposit. Works well for short layovers between trains.

  • Best for: Quick 1‑2 hour meets, no transport needed.

2. Naomi – outcall to waiting taxi/boda (KSh 4,000/hour + KSh 200 transport)

Naomi doesn't have an incall, but she will come to your taxi or boda and direct you to a nearby short‑stay hotel (she knows a few). She handles the negotiation. Very train‑friendly – she's done this dozens of times. Transport fee is just KSh 200 (boda fare). No deposit.

  • Best for: Travelers who don't want to find an incall themselves.

3. Ruth – incall near Acacia Estate (KSh 5,000/hour)

Ruth's apartment is a 7‑minute boda ride from the station (KSh 200). It's in a secure compound with parking. The room is modern – AC, Netflix, private bathroom. She's friendly and used to travelers with luggage. She'll help you book an Uber back to the station or to Nairobi.

  • Best for: Longer stays (3+ hours), overnight.
  • Bonus: She can hold your luggage during the booking if you have a bag.

4. Ivy – outcall to Gateway Mall area (KSh 6,000/hour + KSh 500 transport)

Ivy prefers meeting at a hotel or Airbnb near Gateway Mall. She doesn't do incall. She's classy, speaks good English, and dresses well. She'll arrive by Uber (you pay the fare). She requires a KSh 1,000 deposit for first‑time train travelers (refundable if you cancel 1 hour before arrival).

  • Best for: Travelers who booked a separate hotel room.
  • Note: She does not do quick 1‑hour meets – minimum 2 hours.

5. Zara – 24/7 incall behind the station (KSh 4,500/hour)

Zara is the only one who works odd hours – if your train arrives at 2am, she's awake. Her incall is directly behind the station's back gate (a 3‑minute walk). It's a small room but safe. She's direct, no small talk. Cash only after midnight.

  • Best for: Late‑night train arrivals (11pm – 5am).

Practical tips for SGR travelers

  • Book before your train arrives. Most escorts are available, but pre‑booking saves waiting time.
  • Use the station's free WiFi to WhatsApp – saves your data.
  • Store luggage at the station (left‑luggage counter, KSh 200‑300 per bag). Don't drag suitcases to an incall.
  • Avoid meeting at the station itself – it's crowded, with police and cameras. Walk to a nearby café (e.g., Java at Gateway Mall) then take transport together.
  • Boda from station to Acacia Estate costs KSh 200 – agree on price before getting on.

Madaraka Express schedule (relevant for timing)

Trains from Mombasa arrive at Syokimau at: 8:00am, 1:00pm, 7:00pm. From Nairobi (Nairobi Terminus) to Syokimau is a 15‑min ride, but most travelers come from Mombasa. If you're arriving late (7pm), Zara is your best bet. Morning arrivals (8am) – Lucy or Ruth are available.
