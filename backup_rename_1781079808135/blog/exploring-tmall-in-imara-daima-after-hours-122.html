Exploring T‑Mall in Imara Daima After Hours – 2025 Night Guide

26 May 2025 | Brian Omondi | 5 min read

Complete Guide: For everything on nightlife, safety, and accommodation, see our Ultimate Imara Daima Guide.
Clarification: The official “T‑Mall” is on Langata Road, but locals in Imara Daima often call the Imaara Shopping Mall “T‑Mall”. This guide covers Imaara Mall – the real hub of Imara Daima.

Imara Daima is a quiet, secure middle‑class suburb about 8 km from Nairobi’s CBD. It’s close to JKIA and the SGR station. The main social and retail centre is Imaara Shopping Mall – often called “T‑Mall” by residents. If you’re visiting the area at night, here’s what you need to know.

1. What’s Open After Dark?

Most shops inside Imaara Mall close by 10 PM. However, the anchor tenant, Naivas Supermarket, sometimes stays open later – good for last‑minute shopping. Restaurants like CJ’s, Pizza Inn, and Big Knife serve early evening crowds but usually shut by 10 PM. For true “after hours” (past 10 PM), your options are limited to a few outside food joints or ordering delivery via Uber Eats.

The mall itself is well‑lit and has private security until closing time. After that, the area becomes quiet – it’s residential, not a party zone.

2. Safety at Night in Imara Daima

Imara Daima is generally safe. Most apartments are in gated communities with 24‑hour guards. The main roads (Mombasa Road and the bypass) are well‑lit and busy even at night. However:

  • Avoid walking alone on isolated backstreets or near the railway line after 11 PM.
  • Use ride‑hailing apps (Uber/Bolt) instead of flagging down random taxis.
  • If meeting someone for the first time, choose the mall’s food court or a well‑lit petrol station – never a private residence without verification.

3. Getting to / from Imara Daima at Night

  • Uber / Bolt: Safest option. From JKIA to Imaara Mall costs around KSh 300–500. From Nairobi CBD, expect KSh 600–1,000 depending on traffic.
  • Train (SGR / Commuter Rail): The Imara Daima station is a 5‑minute walk from the mall. The last train to Nairobi usually leaves around 9 PM. Not reliable for late night returns.
  • Matatus (Indimanje Sacco): Run from CBD (Afya Centre) to Imara Daima for KSh 50–100. Frequency drops sharply after 8 PM. Avoid after 10 PM.
  • Boda Boda: Available but not recommended at night – too risky.

4. Nearby Accommodation for Night Visitors

If you’re staying overnight, Imaara Mall is surrounded by secure apartments and Airbnbs. Several offer self check‑in (lockbox) for maximum privacy. Typical rates:

  • Budget Airbnb (basic studio): KSh 3,000–5,000/night
  • Mid‑range (modern, AC, Netflix): KSh 5,000–8,000/night
  • Premium (gated compound, pool/gym): KSh 8,000–12,000/night

Search for “Imaara Mall” or “Imara Daima” on Airbnb and filter for “self check‑in”. That way you avoid any front desk interaction.

5. Final Tips for a Smooth Night

  • Arrive before 9 PM if you want to grab a meal or shop – most places close by 10 PM.
  • Pre‑book your Uber/Bolt when leaving – wait times after midnight can be 10‑15 minutes.
  • Keep a friend updated with your live location if meeting someone new.
  • Carry small notes (KSh 200, 500) – many boda and taxi drivers don’t have change for 1,000.
