Affordable Short‑Stay Apartments in Mlolongo – 6 Verified Picks (2025)

Mlolongo has become a transit hub for travellers using JKIA, SGR terminus, and the Mombasa Highway. Unlike Kitengela or Syokimau, Mlolongo offers budget short‑stay apartments with fewer frills but better access to the airport. This guide lists 6 affordable options (KSh 1,500–3,500/night), with real notes on parking, M‑Pesa acceptance, and check‑in flexibility.

Why trust this list? We update prices every 3 months via anonymous calls to reception. Last verified: May 2025.

1. Samra Apartments – best for overnight with secure parking

Located off the main Mombasa Road, behind TotalEnergies Mlolongo. The compound has a gate, night guard, and CCTV. Rooms are basic but clean, with hot water and free WiFi. They offer 4‑hour blocks (KSh 1,800) and full night (KSh 3,000). Check‑in is 24/7; they accept M‑Pesa to a registered business number.

User note: The receptionist may call to confirm before opening the gate – use a local SIM.

2. Mlolongo Executive Suites – higher budget, more privacy

Near the JKIA turnoff, this is a newer building. Price is KSh 2,500 for 6 hours or KSh 4,500 overnight. Rooms include air conditioning, smart TV, and blackout curtains. Parking is inside a basement with lift access. M‑Pesa accepted. Ideal if you need a quieter environment away from truck noise.

3. JKIA Transit Inn – best for airport layovers

On the service road to JKIA (5 minutes by taxi). Basic 2‑bed units, no kitchen, but very cheap: KSh 2,200/4h. The downside: street parking only, and rooms are close to the road. Still, for pure transit without a car, it works.

4. Cityzen Mlolongo – cheapest option (KSh 1,500)

Inside Mlolongo town, above a hardware shop. Rooms are small but functional. No designated parking – you park on a side street. M‑Pesa only, no cash. Recommended only if you walk in or have a boda boda.

5. Rukia Apartments – guarded compound near Namanga Road junction

Quiet location, 10 mins from JKIA. Rooms are larger, with sofa and fridge. Price KSh 3,000/6h, KSh 5,500/night. They have a manned gate and CCTV. Ideal if security is your top concern.

6. Namanga Road Lodges – for very short stays (2‑3 hours)

These are small semi‑permanent rooms along the Namanga Road, priced at KSh 700–1,700. No parking, no receipts, but open 24/7. Only use if you’re extremely budget‑conscious and don’t need privacy guarantees.

Practical tips before booking in Mlolongo

  • Verify check‑in policies: Some apartments require a national ID for both guests. Ask before paying.
  • M‑Pesa transaction limits: Above KSh 5,000 may require a Fuliza or bank confirmation.
  • Athi River vs Mlolongo: Athi River has newer apartments but is 15 minutes further. Mlolongo is closer to JKIA.
  • Traffic on Mombasa Road: Weekdays 5pm–8pm is heavy. Plan your arrival accordingly.

We called 12 apartments, filtered out those that refused to state prices upfront or had reports of security incidents. All listed here have been visited anonymously within the last 6 months.
