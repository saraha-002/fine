4. Planning Your Overnight – What You Need to Prepare

  • Choose the right accommodation. For incall, ensure her apartment is in a secure estate (e.g., Acacia, Bustani). For outcall, book an Airbnb or hotel with 24‑hour reception and clear visitor policies – see our JKIA hotels guide.
  • Communicate clearly before the night. Discuss start and end times, whether dinner is included, dietary preferences, and any special requests (e.g., massage, role play).
  • Have clean sheets and basic toiletries. If outcall, prepare the bedroom and bathroom. A thoughtful host makes a difference.
  • Prepare payment in advance. Have the full amount in cash (plus a tip if the experience is exceptional) ready before she arrives. For deposits, use M‑Pesa.
  • Stock the fridge. Water, juice, simple snacks, and breakfast items show consideration. If you order dinner, ask about her preferences.

5. Safety & Etiquette for Overnight Bookings

  • Meet briefly before a full overnight if possible. A coffee or drink in a public place helps both parties feel comfortable.
  • Respect each other’s space. Even in an intimate setting, overnight means sharing a room for hours – be polite, and give her a moment alone if needed.
  • Do not pressure for more than agreed. If the night goes well, you can discuss extending the morning or future bookings – but respect boundaries.
  • Keep your phone charged and share your location with a trusted friend. Safety works both ways.
  • Leave a review after the experience. Positive feedback helps other clients and rewards good service.
Timing tip: Most overnight bookings start between 7 pm and 10 pm, ending between 7 am and 10 am. Always confirm the schedule to avoid misunderstandings.
