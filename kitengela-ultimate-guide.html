🚗 Transport & Access

⚠️ Safety & Crime – What You Must Know

Critical warning: Kitengela has experienced a surge in night‑time gang activity. Gangs “Gaza” and “ZZ” operate from around 9 pm, with muggings, car thefts, and violent attacks. Five people were killed in just two months in 2025.

No‑go zones after 9 pm: Miriam Road, Mwireri Lane, Deliverance Road junction, Balozi Road, Kyangombe, Noonkopir.

Safety checklist:

  • Use Uber/Bolt – never walk on side streets.
  • Stick to Namanga Road main strip.
  • Share live location with a friend.
  • Carry small notes (KSh 200, 500).
  • Avoid confrontation – leave if a situation feels tense.
